The idea

Just you and the people you choose.

Most apps ask for your phone number, build a profile, and quietly learn who you talk to. HopNet does the opposite: it collects nothing about you.

  • No sign-up, no phone number, no email.
  • You add friends by scanning a QR code or sharing a link.
  • No ads, no trackers, no strangers.
How it works

How your message travels.

Think of it like handing a sealed letter to a friend.

1

You seal it

Your phone locks the message so only your friend's phone can open it.

2

It goes direct

Whenever you're both online, it goes straight phone-to-phone. No middleman.

3

Or it waits, locked

If your friend is offline, it waits in a locked box until they're back — still sealed.

That locked box is what we call the blind mailbox. It's the only piece of the internet HopNet ever touches — and it can't read a thing.

Our promise

Even we can't read it.

The blind mailbox holds only the sealed, unreadable version of your message. The keys live only on your and your friend's phones.

🔒 Contents: locked, unreadable to us 🏷️ Filed under a random number, not your name 🕗 Auto-deleted within 7 days 🧭 Can't tell who talks to whom

The mailbox sees an anonymous locked parcel arrive and leave. It never learns who you are, who your friend is, or what you said.

The honest bit

Why we use Cloudflare — and why it costs a little.

That blind mailbox has to live somewhere reliable, worldwide. We rent it from Cloudflare, one of the internet's most trusted infrastructure providers, and we chose it precisely because it lets the mailbox stay blind — it only ever holds sealed, anonymous parcels.

Running it isn't free. We'd rather tell you that plainly than hide it, sell your data, or fill the app with ads. HopNet has no ads and no trackers — and it always will.
